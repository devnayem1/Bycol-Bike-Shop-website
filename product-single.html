Product Overview

The Aria SL Aero was developed alongside our sponsored race team over three seasons of wind-tunnel testing and live race data. Every tube profile is shaped to cut drag without adding weight, so you hold your power longer into the closing kilometers of a race.

Full carbon monocoque construction keeps the frame stiff under hard efforts while still absorbing road buzz on long stages. Paired with Dura-Ace Di2 shifting, the Aria SL Aero is built for riders who want a genuine race bike — not a showroom bike.
